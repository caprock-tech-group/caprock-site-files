3. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Terms

3.1 Coverage Hours & Availability

Provider’s standard business hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday, excluding standard US holidays. Support requested outside these hours constitutes Emergency After-Hours Support and is billable at 1.5x the standard rate unless explicitly included in the Client's selected Service Tier.

3.2 Targeted Response Times

Provider utilizes a triage system to prioritize operational impact. Targeted response times during business hours are as follows:
  • Critical (Severity 1): Complete network outage or active security breach. Target Response: 1 Hour.
  • High (Severity 2): Significant operational impairment affecting multiple users. Target Response: 4 Hours.
  • Normal (Severity 3): Standard user requests, password resets, minor bugs. Target Response: Next Business Day.

3.3 The Panhandle Travel Policy

On-site support travel within the city limits of Amarillo and Canyon, TX incurs no trip charge. Travel to locations outside this primary zone will be billed a travel surcharge of $1.50 per mile (round trip) from Provider headquarters, regardless of Service Tier, unless a superseding written addendum is provided.

3.4 Exclusions from Managed Services

The Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) covers maintenance, monitoring, and support of the existing environment as scoped. It does not include: (a) The cost of new hardware or software licensing not explicitly bundled; (b) Major project labor (e.g., server migrations, office moves, complete network rebuilds); (c) Support for proprietary Line of Business software beyond basic vendor liaison services. Major projects will be quoted separately.

3.5 Term and Termination

This Agreement is effective upon execution and operates on a month-to-month basis unless an annual discount term is selected. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Upon termination, Client is responsible for all fees accrued up to the termination date. Provider will cooperatively hand over administrative credentials upon account settlement.
