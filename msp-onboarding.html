MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT (MSA)

Governing Law: State of Texas | Venue: Potter/Randall County

1. Preamble & Scope

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Caprock Technology Group, LLC ("Caprock"), a Texas Limited Liability Company based in Amarillo, and the undersigned entity ("Client").

Purpose: This Agreement governs the provision of Mission Critical Managed IT Services. This Agreement serves as the umbrella for all services selected in Schedule A and the Term commitments in Schedule B.

2. Term & Termination

2.1. Initial Term & Renewal: The "Initial Term" of this Agreement shall be determined by the selection made in Schedule B. Automatic Renewal: Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for subsequent terms of equal length unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.

2.2. Termination for Convenience & Acceleration:

  • Month-to-Month Agreements: May be terminated by Client with thirty (30) days' written notice.
  • Fixed Term Agreements (12, 24, 36 Months): Client acknowledges that the rates and discounts provided in Schedule B are contingent upon the commitment to the full Initial Term. In the event Client terminates this Agreement for convenience prior to the end of the Initial Term, all remaining monthly fees for the remainder of the Term shall immediately become due and payable as a liquidated debt, not as a penalty. (e.g., cancelling a 12-month contract at Month 6 requires immediate payment of the remaining 6 months).

3. Financial & Operational Integrity

3.1. Strategic Onboarding & Standardization Fee (One-Time): To cover the labor and tooling required for initial network mapping, security agent deployment, and technical standardization, a one-time Strategic Onboarding & Standardization Fee of $500.00 is assessed upon execution of this Agreement. Waiver Protocol: This fee may be waived strictly contingent upon the selection of a qualifying Term in Schedule B.

3.2. Automated Anniversary Billing: Client agrees to maintain a valid ACH Authorization or Credit Card on file.

  • Billing Cycle: Service fees are billed in advance on a recurring monthly basis, triggered on the Service Anniversary Date (the day of the month service was activated).
  • Reconciliation: Client billing will automatically adjust each month based on the active user/endpoint count detected by Caprock's auditing tools on the day prior to the Service Anniversary Date.

3.3. Non-Payment & Suspension:

  • Late Fees: Invoices unpaid after ten (10) days are subject to a 1.5% monthly late fee.
  • Suspension: Caprock reserves the right to suspend all services if payment is delinquent by fifteen (15) days. Reactivation following suspension requires a $150.00 administrative fee.

3.4. Support Labor Rates & Inclusions: Under the selected tier, specific labor inclusions apply.

3.5. Coverage Scope Limitation (Device Truing): All Service Tier inclusions, including unlimited remote/on-site support, SLA response times, and discounted labor rates, apply strictly and exclusively to the designated number of Managed Users/Endpoints billed in this Agreement. Technical support requested for unmanaged devices, unauthorized BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) equipment, or endpoints exceeding the active licensed count will be billed at standard non-member emergency rates ($150.00/hr) unless a licensing true-up is executed.

3.6. Exclusions from Managed Services: The Monthly Service Fee covers maintenance, monitoring, and support of the existing environment as scoped. It does not include: (a) The cost of new hardware, parts, or software licensing not explicitly bundled; (b) Major project labor (e.g., server migrations, office moves, complete network rebuilds); (c) Support for proprietary Line of Business software beyond basic vendor liaison services.

4. Liability

4.1. Limitation of Liability: Caprock's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall be strictly limited to the total amount of service fees paid by Client to Caprock during the three (3) months immediately preceding the claim.

4.2. "Shadow IT" Indemnification: Client agrees to indemnify and hold Caprock harmless against any claims or data breaches resulting from hardware, software, or personal devices (BYOD) installed or utilized without the express written vetting of Caprock.
